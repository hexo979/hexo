Math Dive

Seven hard equations. 60 seconds each. Whole-number answers only.

Exact answer: 100 points. Off by 1: 85. Off by 2 to 10: 60. Further off: up to 55, based on how far you are in percent. Answer within the first 8 seconds for the full speed bonus of up to +50% (it fades to zero by 60 seconds), but only if you are within 10 of the answer. Further off, the bonus shrinks: up to +30% if you are within 10%, up to +10% if within 50%, and none beyond that. The same seven equations appear for everyone on the same day.
